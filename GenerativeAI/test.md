A quick use case:
Assume an oraganization has 12 projects going each invoving 10 teams each team
There are 4 product managers hadling 3 projects each 
* Their task is to create PRD in google docs
* Collaborate with technical leads of 10 teams for each project
    * Let lead to collaborate on PRD with questions and answers on document online as the discussion eveloves accepted feautures go into prd and rejected are deleted.
    * Update the plan and timelines as the requirements evolves

The daily difficult tasks of product
1. Create PRD with lot of brainstorming
2. Meeting teams and minutes of meeting
3. Review the questions and update the each section of the document as per the accepted or rejected features.
4. Changed the plan document and times
5. More importantly recollect on what is goining on in each of the 4 projects

Now with AI
1. Start with vage requirments and keep prombting to direct AI to provide insites and in 30min your PRD would be ready
2. Meetings
3. You have googles AI assistant on doc to review and summerize the document and make you easily navigate through accepting and rejecting features and reorganizing PRD appropriatly ( max 30min a day)
4. Ask AI to plan setting a strict end date. It provides you with full plan with timelines (1hr max)
5. Now you do not need to read 100 page PRD to recollect what all changes it has gone through but AI can give you all the answers to give you summary what changes you have done on first itereation, second iteration and so on if you care.

Now 1 product handles 10 projects.
